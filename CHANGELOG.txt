# Changelog

All notable changes to nas-file-manager are documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Back, parent folder and home buttons before the path: back returns to the place shown before in
  the window, up to 50 steps.

### Changed

- The path sits in one bar with the buttons, with arrows between the folders, the current one
  marked, and long names cut with their full name on hover.

## [0.1.24]

### Changed

- Maintenance release.

## [0.1.23]

### Added

- Settings of the details panel: off altogether, or on in the grid view or the list view only.
  Where the panel is off, a click on a folder opens it; a file still opens with a double click.

### Changed

- The texts of the interface, in Italian and English, are impersonal and shorter: no ellipsis,
  colon or semicolon, no names of internal programs, commands or variables.
- The settings use the switches and the number stepper of the shared library, and have no close
  button at their bottom. The settings button is larger.
- The trash shows its note in a banner, empties itself from an icon button, disabled while it
  holds nothing, and says it is empty in a placeholder. The breadcrumb ends with a separator.
- Every field has an id or a name, so that the browser no longer warns about them.

### Removed

- Unused texts (`archives`, `available`, `files`, `items`, `theme`), styles never applied
  (`.danger` of the context menu, `.empty_state`, `.full_width`, `.workspace_heading_row`, the
  short line of the loading skeleton) and comments that only named the markup below them.

## [0.1.22]

### Fixed

- Refuse linked trash directories and operations on the trash root; keep new parent directories
  writable by the storage group.
- Keep deletion dates per file version, preserve existing dates during migration, and date every
  descendant of a deleted directory without resetting sibling ages; expire empty directories too.
- Complete the file picker preview mock so component tests report no unhandled rejection.

### Added

- Folders shared on the local network with Samba (SMB, for Windows and Linux): from the menu of a
  folder, Share on the network opens a window with the permissions (read only, or read and write)
  and the protection with the password of the account. The gateway checks that password and hands
  it to Samba; the application never sees it, and asks for it only for the first protected share.
  A shared folder carries a badge over its icon, and the side menu lists them under Shared. The
  list is in the database of the application (`api/shares`) and goes to the launcher at every
  change and at start (`PUT /api/apps/self/shares`); a renamed, moved or deleted folder takes its
  share along. The manifest asks for `permissions.shares` and the Debian package `samba`.
- A search index of the disks (network shares excluded) in the database of the application: the
  search, of the Explorer and of the launcher, finds any part of a name at once and everywhere,
  without the old limits of depth and of files read. Its first build shows among the operations of
  the NAS, and until then the search walks the disk as before. Each folder is then watched with
  inotify (new dependency `notify`, the same as nas-backup-manager), and at start the folders whose
  time changed are read again. Past the inotify limit of the user the extra folders are read again
  every 10 minutes, and a notification says when the watches reach 80% of the limit and when they
  pass it. The manifest sets `run.background`: the Explorer starts with the NAS and stays on with
  its window closed, at the lower priority of the background.
- A trash for each disk, on by default: a deletion moves the file or folder to
  `<disk>/.nas-trash` with the tree it came from, one version for each path (a new one replaces
  the old). The side menu has Trash, with the deleted folders by disk, their size and the time they
  went in: Restore (asking before replacing what took the path meanwhile), Delete for good, Empty.
  Settings, General tab: the trash on or off, the days before an item leaves (30, checked once a
  day) and Empty trash. The time of a deletion is the ctime of the disk, or the one the Explorer
  keeps for its own deletions and for disks without a reliable ctime (exFAT, NTFS). The files
  deleted from a network share go to the same trash, through the `recycle` module of Samba, when
  the launcher knows it (`recycle` in `GET /api/apps/self/shares`). The trash is never listed,
  searched or indexed. The `trash` capability (`/internal/trash`) lets the Cleanup app empty it.
- The `cache` capability (`/internal/cache`, for the launcher only): the previews and the search
  index, with their size and items, emptied on request; the index is built again at once.

### Changed

- The files of `assets/`, with a hash of their content in the name, are served with
  `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, the page with `no-store` as before: the browser keeps
  the scripts and the styles between visits instead of asking for them again through the gateway
  and the launcher.
- Previews of the images: two sizes (256 px for the lists and the grid, 640 px for the details),
  JPEG (PNG only when some of the image is transparent), turned as the camera said (EXIF), never
  enlarged, at most two decoded at once. They stay on the NAS up to a room chosen in the settings
  (Previews, from 100 MB to 2 GB, 2 GB by default, `api/previews/cache`), the ones read least
  recently going first, under a name that no longer changes with the compiler. The browser asks
  only for the previews on the screen and keeps them between visits (their address changes with
  the file). The previews made before are made again once.
- Cut stays inside the NAS: it no longer writes the paths to the clipboard of the system. A move
  between two disks, which a rename cannot do, copies and then removes the original; a folder is
  never moved inside itself. The scissors and paste icons of the menu follow the others.
- An empty folder, the favorites, the shared folders and a search with no result each show a
  symbol, a title and what to do (`EmptyPlaceholder`, the same as the file picker).
- Going from a folder to the favorites or the shared folders and back to all the files returns to
  that folder. The items of the side menu sit closer together.

- The file picker: its title and the cross that cancels it on one row (Escape cancels too); back,
  a home button and the path as a trail of `/` on the left, search, new folder or file and the
  views on the right; the button says Select. An empty folder, or a search that finds nothing,
  shows a symbol and says what to do.
- The table of the documents is `STRICT`. An existing database does not open: install from
  scratch.
- The launcher bridge, the shared styles, the stored documents and the build, package and release
  scripts come from the shared library `@valeriogc/nas-base-app`; only the package smoke test with
  its temporary storage stays in `scripts/`.

## [0.1.21]

### Changed

- Settings and favorites are kept by the backend, in a SQLite database of the application
  (`api/documents/preferences` and `api/documents/favorites`), never the launcher's: every browser
  that opens the NAS sees the same ones. What this browser kept before is carried over once.
- One interface on every screen: the separate mobile layout (with its categories of files) is gone,
  and on a phone the explorer is the desktop one, with the side menu sliding in from the button of
  the header, folders and archives opened with a tap, no dragging, and the actions of a file in its
  details. The dialogs fit inside the margin of the screen.
- The settings: General (confirmation before deleting, free space), Previews, File list, and
  Appearance only outside the launcher, which sets theme and language itself (the tab was empty
  there). The confirmation before deleting is now honored.
- In the frame of the launcher the application keeps the rules of its old sandbox by itself
  (`src/services/frameGuard.ts`, called first in `main.ts`): no window of its own, no
  `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` dialog, no navigation of the page that holds it. The launcher dropped
  the sandbox, which on its origin was no wall and made the browser warn: nothing else changes.
- The file picker: back and search as icons, a smaller search field like the explorer's, then a
  divider and the commands to create a folder (and a file, when picking files) in the folder
  being browsed.
- The file picker switches between the list (by default) and a grid of previews, says when a
  folder has nothing to show, and names the disks by their name instead of their id.
- The focus ring of the search field goes around the whole field.
- The text of the primary buttons follows the theme (`--on-accent`), readable on the bright
  accent of the dark theme.

### Fixed

- Nothing asks the browser any more for a name, a folder or a confirmation (`prompt`, `confirm`),
  which inside the launcher answered nothing: the Copy of the details panel opens the window of
  the move in its copy version, and in the mobile layout Rename and New folder open the window of
  the name, as on the desktop. Both layouts share these windows (`useNameDialog`,
  `useTransferDialog`).
